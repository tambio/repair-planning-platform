# Existing System Comparison

## 1. Introduction
This document compares existing systems for renovation planning and cost control. The goal is to show why my web platform is needed.

## 2. Systems to Compare

1. **Смета.ру** — Russian online service for cost calculation.
2. **Гранд-Смета** — professional software for construction estimates.
3. **PlanRadar** — platform for construction project management.
4. **Buildertrend** — platform for home builders and remodelers.

2. Material list
3. Work scheduling
4. AI planning
5. Receipt tracking
6. Expense analytics (plan vs fact)

## 5. Analysis

### Смета.ру and Гранд-Смета
These systems are good for cost calculation. But they do not plan work and do not track real expenses. They are made for professional estimators, not for private builders.

### PlanRadar and Buildertrend
These are strong platforms for construction companies. They have planning and analytics. But they are expensive and too complex for small private teams. They also do not have AI planning or receipt OCR.

## 6. Why Our Platform Is Needed

No existing system covers all needs of private builders and clients:

- Cost calculation and material list — yes in some systems.
- Receipt and categorization — no system has it.
- Plan vs fact analytics for budget and time — only in expensive corporate systems.
- Client access — only in expensive systems.

Our platform fills this gap. It is simple, affordable, and uses AI to automate routine tasks.

## 7. Conclusion

The comparison shows that existing systems are either too narrow or too expensive. A new intelligent web platform for private builders and clients is needed.
